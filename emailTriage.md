## Intro section

### H2: What is AI email triage?

Email triage is the job of deciding what each incoming email is, how urgent it is, and who should deal with it. In most businesses that job falls to a person who opens the shared inbox every morning and works through it by hand – reading, forwarding, filing and flagging.

AI email triage hands that first pass to artificial intelligence. Instead of rigid Outlook rules that match a keyword or a sender, an AI model reads the email the way a person would. It understands that "the invoice you sent is wrong" is a billing dispute, that "our site is down" is urgent, and that a supplier newsletter can wait. It then takes action: categorising, moving, flagging, creating a task, logging a record or alerting the right team.

The result is a shared inbox that sorts itself, faster response times on the emails that count, and staff who spend their day on work rather than on the inbox.

## Section: Three ways to triage email with AI

### H2: Three ways to automate email triage in Microsoft 365

There is no single "right" way to triage email with AI. The best approach depends on your inbox volume, how many people share the mailbox, what should happen to each email once it is sorted, and which Microsoft licences you already own. We work across all three.

#### Card 1 – Copilot in Outlook (built-in Microsoft tools)

**Best for:** Individuals and managers drowning in their own inbox.

If your team already has Microsoft 365 Copilot licences, much of the personal triage is already switched on – it just needs configuring. Copilot's **Prioritise** feature scores new mail as high, normal or low priority as it arrives, and it can be taught your own rules in plain English, such as emails from key clients or those mentioning a particular project. Copilot can also carry out triage actions on request – flag, pin, archive, mark as read – across several emails at once.

Microsoft is now extending Copilot in Outlook into agentic territory, where it works through the inbox continuously: surfacing what needs a reply, drafting follow-ups and suggesting rules.

- No build required – configuration and training only
- Works per person, on each user's own inbox
- Requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence per user
- Limited control over what happens _after_ an email is prioritised

We help you roll this out properly: licence planning, writing effective prioritisation instructions, and training staff to use it.

#### Card 2 – Power Automate with AI Builder (the Power Platform route)

**Best for:** Shared mailboxes such as info@, accounts@, support@ or orders@.

This is the workhorse approach for team inboxes. A Power Automate cloud flow watches a shared mailbox, and each new email is passed to an AI Builder prompt or classification model. The model returns a category, a priority and any key details it can extract – a customer number, an invoice amount, a due date. The flow then does the rest: moves the email to the right folder, applies an Outlook category, posts an alert to a Teams channel, adds a row to SharePoint or Excel, or creates a record in Dataverse.

- Predictable, rule-based flow with AI doing the "reading"
- One flow can triage an entire shared mailbox for the whole team
- Every decision can be logged for reporting and audit
- Licensed through Power Automate rather than a Copilot licence for every user

Learn more about our [Power Automate and AI Builder consulting](https://www.powerplatformexperts.com.au/services/microsoft-power-platform/microsoft-power-automate).

#### Card 3 – Custom AI agents (Copilot Studio)

**Best for:** Complex inboxes where triage is only the first step of a bigger process.

When sorting the email is not enough, we build a custom AI agent to handle complex tasks. An event trigger wakes the agent whenever a new email lands. The agent reads the message, decides what it is, and then acts across your entire Microsoft 365 environment – looking up the customer in Dataverse or your CRM, checking an order in SharePoint, opening a ticket, drafting a reply for a person to approve, updating a Planner task, or handing the job to another agent.

- Reasons over the email and your business data, not just the email
- Shares information across Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Excel, Dataverse, Dynamics 365 and third-party systems through connectors
- Human-in-the-loop approval before anything leaves the business
- Billed on usage through Copilot Credits, so cost scales with volume

## Section: How it works

### H2: How AI email triage works

_(Numbered – this is sequential)_

1. **An email arrives** – in a personal inbox or a shared mailbox such as accounts@ or support@.
2. **AI reads the message** – the subject, body and, where needed, attachments are passed to a language model that understands intent, not just keywords.
3. **The email is classified** – into your own business categories (for example: new enquiry, quote request, complaint, invoice, supplier update, spam) with a priority level.
4. **Key details are extracted** – names, account numbers, amounts, dates and deadlines are pulled out as structured data.
5. **Action is taken** – the email is moved, categorised, assigned or escalated, and the right person is alerted in Outlook or Teams.
6. **Everything is logged** – every decision is recorded so you can report on volumes, response times and accuracy, and fine-tune the categories over time.

## Section: Security and governance

### H2: Secure, governed and in your control

Email is full of personal and commercially sensitive information, so how AI handles it matters. Every solution we build is designed with governance in mind:

- **Your data stays in your tenant.** Unlike many third-party AI email tools that connect to your mailbox from outside, our solutions run on Microsoft's own platform under your existing Microsoft 365 security, compliance and data loss prevention policies.
- **Least-privilege access.** Flows and agents only get access to the mailboxes and data they genuinely need, and triggers are narrowed so they only fire on relevant mail.
- **Human in the loop.** AI can draft, suggest and sort – but anything that goes out to a customer, or changes a record of consequence, can require a person's approval first.
- **Transparent and auditable.** Every classification is logged, so you can see why an email was sorted the way it was and correct it.
- **Privacy-aware design.** We design with the Australian Privacy Principles in mind and document how automated decisions are made – increasingly important as Australian privacy obligations around automated decision-making tighten.

## FAQ

_(Mark up with FAQPage structured data)_

**What is AI email triage?**
AI email triage uses artificial intelligence to read incoming emails, work out what each one is about and how urgent it is, and then sort, route or act on it automatically. It replaces the manual first pass through an inbox that someone would otherwise do by hand.

**How is AI email triage different from Outlook rules?**
Outlook rules match fixed conditions such as a sender, a word in the subject line, or an address. AI triage understands meaning and intent, so it can tell a complaint from a compliment, or an urgent request from a routine one, even when the wording is different every time.

**Can Microsoft Copilot triage my email?**
Yes. With a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence, Outlook can prioritise new mail as high, normal or low and perform triage actions such as flagging and archiving on request. It is designed for personal inboxes. For shared mailboxes and business processes, Power Automate or a custom Copilot Studio agent is usually a better fit.

**Can AI triage a shared mailbox?**
Yes. Shared mailboxes such as info@, accounts@ and support@ are where AI triage delivers the biggest time savings. We typically use a Power Automate flow or a Copilot Studio agent that monitors the shared mailbox and processes every new email automatically.

**Do I need a Copilot licence for every user?**
Not necessarily. Copilot in Outlook is licensed per user, but a Power Automate flow or Copilot Studio agent running against a shared mailbox is licensed differently, so a whole team can benefit without every person needing Copilot. We'll help you find the most cost-effective option.

**Will AI reply to emails automatically?**
Only if you want it to. Most businesses start with AI sorting and drafting replies for a person to review and send. Fully automatic replies can be enabled for low-risk, repetitive messages once you're confident in the results.

**How accurate is AI email classification?**
Modern language models are very good at understanding email, particularly when categories are clearly defined and described. We test against your real historical emails before go-live, log every decision, and refine the categories so accuracy improves over time.

**Is my email data secure?**
Our solutions are built on Microsoft 365 and the Power Platform, so your email is processed under your existing Microsoft security, compliance and data loss prevention controls rather than being sent to a separate third-party AI service.

**Can AI triage work with our CRM or other systems?**
Yes. Through Microsoft connectors, triaged emails can create or update records in Dynamics 365, Dataverse, SharePoint, Excel and many third-party systems, and we can build custom connectors where needed.

**How long does it take to set up AI email triage?**
Configuring Copilot prioritisation can take a few days including training. A shared-mailbox Power Automate solution is typically a short project, while a custom AI agent that acts across several systems takes longer. We'll give you a clear estimate after the initial consultation.
